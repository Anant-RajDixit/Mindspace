Responsible use

Purpose

Mindspace is an educational project that shows a machine-learning model in action next to simple views of your own inputs.

Not a medical tool

It does not diagnose, treat or assess any health condition, and should not be used to make health decisions. If you are worried about your wellbeing, please speak with a qualified professional.

Model limitations

The model predicts Mental_Health_Score from 12 inputs, including self-reported stress level. It was trained on 4,998 student records aged 18 to 24, so inputs are limited to the ranges it has seen. It describes patterns in that dataset. It does not show that any habit causes a score, and it may not apply to you.

Held-out test results (25% random split): R² 0.891, MAE 0.325, RMSE 0.437. These are measured on the same dataset, so they do not guarantee accuracy for real people. Countries outside the 10 most common in the data are grouped as "Other".

Privacy

Your answers are sent to the server only to compute the prediction. This app does not ask for your name or contact details and does not save your answers.

Mindspace is an educational ML demo, not a medical or diagnostic tool.
